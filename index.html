FAQ

What is an MCP server?

A small program giving your AI one specific skill — reading files, checking a database, sending a message — safely and only on request.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Search or browse, pick what you need, check Guide for the short setup.

Is my data safe?

Nothing that changes or sends data runs without your confirmation first.

Which AI assistants work with these?

Any MCP-compatible assistant — Claude, Antigravity, and others.
